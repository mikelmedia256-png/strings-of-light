About

Claire Gaxiola

A violin player based in the Southeast, Claire toured with the Samford University Orchestra during college and has since played weddings, galas, sacred services, and memorials — along with the roofless stone ruins of a chapel in Guatemala.

Her repertoire moves between contemporary, sacred, and classical, and she is as comfortable holding a room quiet during a processional as she is carrying a reception through the evening.

For Planners

The logistics, answered

Everything you would otherwise have to email and wait for.

How far will you travel?

Claire is based in the Southeast. Travel within [[ TRAVEL RADIUS ]] is included in the quoted rate; beyond that, travel is quoted at cost. Destination events are welcome.

Do you bring your own sound?

Yes. Claire arrives with her own amplification, suitable for gatherings up to [[ GUEST COUNT ]]. For larger rooms she will coordinate directly with your AV team and can run through a house system instead.

What do you need from the venue?

A level surface roughly 6 × 6 feet, one armless chair, and access to power within reach. Outdoors, shade or cover is required — direct sun and rain both damage the instrument.

Can we request specific songs?

Yes. Requests are welcome and included. A custom arrangement of a song that does not yet exist for violin — a first dance, a processional — is available for [[ FEE ]].

How far in advance should we book?

Peak wedding dates in spring and fall are typically claimed [[ LEAD TIME ]] in advance. Corporate and short-notice dates can often be accommodated — ask.

What happens in bad weather, or if Claire falls ill?

Outdoor events need a covered contingency confirmed in advance; Claire will help you plan it. In the event of illness or emergency, she maintains a network of vetted musicians and will arrange a comparable replacement at no additional cost to you.

How do we hold a date?

A signed agreement and a [[ DEPOSIT % ]] retainer hold the date. The balance is due [[ TERMS ]]. Invoicing, W-9, and certificates of insurance are available on request.
